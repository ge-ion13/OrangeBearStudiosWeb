RideCARE

A smart vehicle management app designed to help you track expenses, maintenance, and important reminders in one place.

Coming soon on Google Play

RideCARE is a vehicle management app built to simplify how you track and maintain your car. From logging expenses to managing service reminders, the app focuses on clarity, organization, and making everyday car management easier.


Expense Tracking

Log and organize all car-related expenses, including maintenance, repairs, and other costs, in a clear and structured way.

Smart Reminders

Set reminders for services, inspections, and important dates so nothing is missed.

Fuel Tracking

Track fuel usage and monitor efficiency over time with simple and consistent logging.

Organized History

Access a complete history of your vehicle data, making it easy to review, update, and manage everything in one place.
