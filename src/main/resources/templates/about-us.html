About Orange Bear Studios

Orange Bear Studios is an independent software studio focused on creating thoughtful, and user-focused digital experiences.


Founded through a passion for creativity and independent development, the studio was built with a simple philosophy: create products with purpose, attention to detail, and value.


Throughout the development process, the studio maintains full creative freedom, and it allows ideas to evolve naturally from concept to release.


From mobile applications to interactive experiences, every project is approached with a strong emphasis on quality, usability, and meaningful design.

Apps

Useful apps built to solve everyday problems through clean design and practical functionality.

Games

Games designed to be accessible, engaging, and enjoyable for everyone.

User Experience

Every product is built with a strong focus on clarity, consistency, and user-friendly design.

More projects are in development, with future releases planned across both apps and games. Follow us on social media to find out more.
